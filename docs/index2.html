High Grade (HG) models are typically 1/144th scale and are relatively simple to put together. They are the cheapest (outside of entry grade) and least detailed.

Real Grade (RG) models are also typically 1/144th scale but are much more complicated to put together and are some of the most complex models that Bandai produces. They are a fan favorite line due to thier incredible detail and excellent posability.

Master Grade (MG) models are typically 1/100th scale, and are usually right in between high and real grade kits in terms of complexity. This is my personal favorite line just becuase of how large they are.
